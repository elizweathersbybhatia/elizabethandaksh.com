The Indian Celebration — Day 1

FEBRUARY 23, 2027 · INDIA

11:00 AM – 1:00 PM

Haldi Ceremony

A vibrant, joyful ritual where turmeric paste is applied to the bride and groom for blessings of prosperity and beauty. Wear yellow — and nothing too precious, as things will get colorful! A bath follows the ceremony.

1:00 PM – 3:30 PM

Welcome Lunch

A relaxed welcome lunch for arriving guests. Hotel check-in begins at 2:00 PM. Casual attire is perfectly fine.

3:30 PM – 6:00 PM

Rest & Refresh

Free time to settle in, freshen up, and get ready for the evening festivities.

6:00 PM – 10:00 PM

Cocktails & Dinner

An elegant evening of cocktails and dinner under the stars. Western or Indian formal attire — think stunning and festive. Outdoor music ends at 10 PM due to forest area regulations.

10:00 PM Onward

Sangeet & After-Party

The party moves indoors! The Sangeet is a night of music, dance performances, and celebration. Get ready to dance the night away.

The Indian Celebration — Day 2

FEBRUARY 24, 2027 · INDIA

12:00 PM – 2:00 PM

Pheras & Jai Mala

The wedding ceremony — the sacred pheras (circling the holy fire) and jai mala (exchange of garlands). A beautiful blend of ritual and emotion. Formal Indian or Western attire.

2:00 PM – 3:30 PM

Lunch & Cocktails

A celebratory lunch following the ceremony, complete with cocktails and plenty of joy.

2:30 PM – 4:00 PM

Rest & Change

Time to rest and change into your evening outfit for the reception.

4:00 PM – 5:00 PM

Sera Bandi & Baraat Assembly

The groom's procession prepares to begin! The Sera Bandi (turban-tying ceremony) takes place, and guests gather for the grand baraat.

5:00 PM – 6:30 PM

Baraat

The groom's grand procession with music, dancing, and celebration. Timed with the golden hour as the sun sets around 6 PM — pure magic.

6:30 PM – 10:00 PM

Reception & Dinner

The grand reception and dinner celebration. Indian or Western formal attire welcome — dress to impress!

Post Dinner

After-Party

For the night owls — the celebrations continue in comfortable clothes. Come as you are and let's keep the party going!

Style Guide

What to Wear

Five outfits across two unforgettable days. Here's your guide to dressing for each celebration.

01
Haldi Ceremony
Day 1 · Morning

Men: Yellow kurta pajama — keep it simple and fun.

Women: Anything yellow! A salwar kameez, ghagra choli, or any festive yellow outfit.

Note: Turmeric stains everything — don't wear anything you're too attached to!

Indian · Casual
02
Cocktails & Dinner
Day 1 · Evening

Men: Western or Indian formal. Jodhpuri suits, blazers, or classic suits all work beautifully.

Women: Lehenga, gown, or cocktail dress — this is your moment to shine. It will be cool in the evening, so plan a wrap or shawl.

Formal · Western or Indian
03
Pheras & Jai Mala
Day 2 · Ceremony

Men: Formal Indian (sherwani, kurta) or Western suit — keeping in mind international guests.

Women: Formal saree, lehenga, or elegant Western dress. Rich, vibrant colors are encouraged.

Formal · Indian or Western
04
Reception & Dinner
Day 2 · Evening

Men & Women: Indian or Western formal — this is the grand finale celebration. Think glamorous and festive.

With international guests joining, both styles are warmly welcomed.

Formal · Glamorous
05
After-Party
Day 2 · Late Night

Change into something comfortable — jeans, kurta, whatever lets you dance freely!

The party continues for those who want to keep celebrating.

Casual · Comfy

Weather & Climate

February in India brings pleasant weather: temperatures range from 9°C to 23°C (48°F to 73°F). Evenings can be cool, so bring a light jacket or shawl. Sunrise is around 6:45 AM and sunset at 6:00 PM.

Helpful Tips

  • Indian visa may be required — check well in advance
  • Comfortable shoes for outdoor events
  • Sunscreen and sunglasses for daytime celebrations
  • A warm layer for cool evenings
